Why Conventional Medical Tools Fail
01 · Cloud Telemedicine Drops Offline
Requires active Mission Control uplink — unavailable during Solar Particle Event (SPE) blackouts and outer transit.
02 · Generative LLMs Hallucinate
Probabilistic chatbots cannot guarantee deterministic NASA-STD-3001 limits when seconds matter in acute triage.
03 · Generic Population Averages Mislead
Every astronaut has a distinct cardiovascular baseline — triage must score personal delta, not averages.
No Cloud Uplink
NASA HRP HAZARDS 01 & 03
Space Radiation + Distance from Earth
Acute SPE spikes + GCR cumulative dose against 600 mSv career limit with zero real-time ground flight surgeon.
No LLM Guessing
NASA HRP HAZARDS 02 & 04
Isolation + Altered Gravity Fields
Months of confinement drive sleep drift and behavioral fatigue, alongside bone density & cardiovascular deconditioning.
Personal Baseline
NASA HRP HAZARD 05
Hostile / Closed Environments
Scrubber faults push ppCO₂ > 3.0 mmHg; unit mix-ups (% vs mmHg, mSv vs µSv/h) corrupt manual logs.
